How It Works
How It Works

Touch. Open. Choose.

ecompass uses NFC (Near Field Communication) to connect the physical object to the digital experience. No pairing, no Bluetooth, no app install.

1

Tap

Hold your phone's NFC area against the ecompass surface for 1–2 seconds.

2

Open

The browser opens the ecompass experience page directly. No app store. No install prompt.

3

Choose

Reset, Focus, or Reflect. Each is a complete, self-contained experience under 2 minutes.

4

Return

Close the page. Put the compass back. The ritual is done. You've reset.

No data is collected. The NFC tag contains only a URL. It cannot read your phone, access your data, or store any information about you. Works with iPhone XS+ and most Android phones (2015+). QR code fallback included for any phone.

FAQ
FAQ

Questions we expect.

Do I need an app?
No. ecompass is designed to work with your phone's default browser through NFC. No app install, no account creation, no data collection.
Which phones work with NFC?
Most Android phones from 2015 onward. iPhone XS and later (requires tapping the back of the phone to the compass). For phones without NFC, a QR code on the base of the compass serves as the fallback trigger.
Is this a meditation product?
Not exactly. ecompass can support calm, focus, and reflection, but we are not positioning it as a formal meditation system or a therapeutic tool. It's better understood as a desk ritual object for the small, everyday moments between tasks.
Does it collect any data?
No. The NFC tag contains only a URL. It cannot read your phone, access your contacts, location, or any personal information. The digital experience has no accounts, no tracking, and no data storage.
What if I want a refund?
We offer a 30-day return policy for undamaged units. Contact us directly — not through a bot — and we'll sort it out. We want first-edition backers to feel genuinely good about their purchase.
When will it ship?
We are targeting Q3 2026 for first shipments. Early Bird backers ship first, in the order their pledges are confirmed. We'll send tracking info and updates as each batch goes out.
Is the NFC tag rewritable?
Yes. The NFC tag embedded in ecompass is rewritable. If we release a new experience URL in the future, backers will be able to update it. Instructions will be provided.
What if I lose my compass?
The experience URL is unique to your unit. If you lose it, contact us — we'll help you get a replacement or redirect your experience. We're building this for the long term, not for one-time transactions.
