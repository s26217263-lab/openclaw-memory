04 · Why Now Morning

Morning — 在一天开始之前，先把自己拿回来

Morning 场景承担的不是“早起自律”叙事，而是把 ecompass 放进一个更真实的清晨空隙：第一条消息、第一份待办、第一段滚动开始之前，用户还有机会决定今天如何开始。

场景目标

  • 让用户理解产品属于一天开始的第一个安静时刻
  • 强调 begin gently / start with intention
  • 建立清晨桌面 ritual 的可信语境

视觉关键词

  • soft morning light
  • clean air
  • calm beginning
  • tactile object
Before the day begins, begin with yourself. A small morning ritual to pause, reset, and choose how the day starts.

Natural sunlight enters from a nearby window, casting gentle warm highlights and soft shadows across the desk. The brass compass is the clear focal point, positioned slightly off-center with a tactile, refined, softly aged metallic finish. 05 · Why Now Work

Work — 不再加压，而是把注意力轻轻拉回来

Work 场景不讲高效神话，也不讲职场炫耀，而是聚焦一个更常见的中段时刻：人已经在桌前，但注意力开始漂移。ecompass 在这里的角色，不是刺激你更卷，而是给你一个 physical reset trigger。

场景目标

  • 说明产品适合进入真实工作桌面
  • 把价值点锚定在 return to focus，而不是 productivity hype
  • 建立 calm work rhythm 的场景说服

视觉关键词

  • focus reset
  • desk presence
  • quiet concentration
  • grounded, restrained
When your attention drifts, return with one touch. A quiet desk ritual that helps you reset and come back to what matters now.
An editorial product lifestyle photograph of a brass compass on a modern work desk during the middle of the day. The desk is clean but actively used: a notebook, a pen, a few paper cards, and subtle traces of work are present. The compass is the visual focal point, placed near the front-middle area of the composition. It feels grounded, tactile, and intentional. Lighting is natural daylight or soft indirect studio daylight, slightly cooler than a morning scene but still balanced and calm.
06 · Why Now Evening

Evening — 给结束一天这件事，一个更轻的入口

Evening 场景讲的不是睡眠、疗愈或神秘感，而是一天收束的动作。工作停下了，心还没收回；外部噪音退下去了，内在噪音才刚刚浮上来。ecompass 在这里扮演的是一个 gentle closing ritual。

场景目标

  • 建立产品在 end-of-day 时刻的现实价值
  • 强调 close the loop / let the day land
  • 避免所有神秘疗愈行业模板感

视觉关键词

  • quiet closure
  • warm twilight
  • reflective
  • intimate desk scene
End the day with a quieter kind of attention. A simple evening ritual to pause, reflect, and let the day settle.
A refined editorial lifestyle photograph of a brass compass placed on a desk in the evening, lit by a warm desk lamp and subtle twilight in the background. The desk feels lived-in but calm: a closed notebook, a ceramic mug, a pen, and a few neatly resting paper cards suggest the end of a day rather than active work. The brass compass is the clear focal point, softly catching warm light with a tactile, elegant metallic finish. The atmosphere is intimate, quiet, and reflective.
07 · Kickstarter IA

Kickstarter 页面骨架 / 信息架构

首版页面的任务，不是讲大故事，而是在最短时间内让支持者理解三件事：这是什么、为什么有意义、为什么值得支持。页面要以体验、场景与真实使用语境为主线，而不是用技术炫耀或神秘包装来偷说服。

页面主结构

  • Hero
  • Why Now
  • What ecompass Is
  • How It Works
  • Materials & Form
  • Experience Flow
  • Rewards
  • Timeline / Risks / FAQ / CTA

说服路径

  • 看见物件 → 理解定位
  • 场景共鸣 → 明白运作
  • 感受到材质价值 → 接受回报结构
  • 建立对风险与节奏的信任
真正的说服，不来自夸张概念，而来自：产品定义清楚、使用场景可信、执行路径诚实。
08 · Kickstarter Copy

Kickstarter 首版汇报文案

这部分提供的是首版汇报级文案，不是最终上线英文定稿。目标是先把产品语言站稳：它是一个 tactile desk ritual object，通过触碰进入一个 lightweight digital experience，帮助用户 pause, reset, focus, reflect。

推荐标题 / 副标题

  • Title — ecompass — A Tactile Desk Ritual for Pause, Reset, and Focus
  • Subtitle — A thoughtfully designed desk object that connects touch with a lightweight digital experience.

核心文案方向

  • 不从更多功能开始，而从更少干扰开始
  • 不许诺 transformation，只提供 meaningful pause
  • 把 Why Now 讲成 everyday life 的 texture
We are not trying to build a louder product. We are trying to build a quieter, more intentional one.
